Privacy notice

Last updated: 21 August 2026

This page explains what we collect when you pre-order something, why we need it, and what you can ask us at any time.

Who handles your data

Data controller
SunnyCreations
Privacy contact
sunnycreations@gmail.com

What we collect

Only what a pre-order needs: first name, surname, email address and phone number, along with the items, sizes and quantities you choose. We never collect payment details: payment happens on PayPal or by bank transfer, away from this site, and all we record is whether an order has been paid.

Why we use it

To handle your pre-order
To prepare your quote, email it to you, contact you about collection and keep the event's accounts. Without this we cannot take the order: the legal basis is taking steps at your request before a contract.
To tell you about updates (optional)
Only if you ticked that box, we email you when the price list changes or new items arrive. The legal basis is your consent, and you can withdraw it whenever you like.

Who else sees it

Nobody, other than the technical providers that make the service work, acting on our behalf:

Supabase
Stores the orders. The database is hosted in the European Union (Frankfurt).
Resend
Sends the quote emails, from the European region (Ireland).
GitHub Pages
Hosts this page. It never receives your order details.

Supabase, Resend and GitHub are US companies: where processing involves a transfer outside the European Union, it relies on the standard contractual clauses approved by the European Commission. Your data is never sold or passed to third parties for advertising.

How long we keep it

Orders are kept until the event they belong to is closed, and for 12 months afterwards, long enough to close the accounts and handle any returns. If you consented to updates, your email address stays until you withdraw that consent.

What you can ask for

At any time you can ask to see the data we hold about you, to correct it, to have it deleted, to restrict its use, to object to the processing, or to receive it in a format another service can read. Write to sunnycreations@gmail.com: we answer within a month.

If you believe your data is being mishandled, you may complain to the Italian supervisory authority, the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali, or to the authority in your own country.

Cookies

This site uses no tracking cookies and no analytics. Your browser only keeps, locally, the language you picked and your pre-order in progress, so you do not lose it on a reload: neither leaves your device.
